A student-led project for our college

Hi, I'm Himanshu Thakur.

I'm a BCA student, and I created this learning hub to make it easier for students in my college to find course roadmaps, track syllabus progress, and access useful learning resources in one place.

Why I built it

Made for students, shaped by student needs.

This is an independent student project to help BCA students study at their own pace. It brings year-wise subjects, module checklists, curated video playlists, notes, and practice links together so it is easier to know what to study next.

The goal is simple: make helpful resources easier to discover and keep learning a little more organized.
