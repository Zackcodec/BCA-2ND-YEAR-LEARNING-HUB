Privacy policy

Information: If you create an account, the service uses your email address and the profile details you choose to provide (such as your display name, bio, and social links). It also stores your course progress and learning-activity dates to provide the dashboard, streaks, and personal completion certificates.

Storage and sharing: Account information is handled by Supabase for sign-in and cloud storage. Database access is restricted to the signed-in account by the configured access policies. The app does not intentionally publish student profiles. Your chosen social links open third-party sites, where their own privacy terms apply.

Browser and third parties: The app may store sign-in session data and simple preferences in your browser. Google sign-in, YouTube embeds, fonts/icons, the QR display library, GitHub Pages hosting, and any payment/contribution provider may receive technical data under their own policies. The UPI QR is encoded in your browser; your UPI ID is not sent to the QR library provider. The app does not sell student profile or progress data.

Choices and requests: You can edit profile details or log out from the app. Account deletion is not currently self-service; for a privacy or deletion request, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub page. Do not post passwords or private account details publicly. Provider backups or records may remain for a limited period or where law requires.

For payment, cancellation, or refund requests, contact the provider or UPI app/bank involved and check its terms before paying.

A student-led project for our college

Hi, I'm Himanshu Thakur.

I'm a BCA student, and I created this learning hub to make it easier for students in my college to find course roadmaps, track syllabus progress, and access useful learning resources in one place.

Why I built it

Made for students, shaped by student needs.

This is an independent student project to help BCA students study at their own pace. It brings year-wise and semester-wise subjects, module checklists, curated video playlists, notes, and practice links together so it is easier to know what to study next.

The goal is simple: make helpful resources easier to discover and keep learning a little more organized.
